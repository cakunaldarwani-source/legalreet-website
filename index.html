    Get a written quote

    A LegalReet expert calls back, confirms scope and eligibility, and sends a fixed fee in writing.

  3. 3

    Upload documents

    Your application is prepared, filed and chased with the department by the same expert who quoted you.

    Approvals arrive digitally, along with the recurring due dates that now apply to your business.

20Oct 26

Quarterly TDS and TCS statements for July–September due by 31 October 2026

The tax audit extension does not cover TDS returns. Q2 statements must still be filed by 31 October to avoid daily late fees.

15Oct 26

LLP Form 8 due 30 October; company AOC-4 and MGT-7 run from the AGM date

LLPs must file their statement of accounts and solvency for FY 2025-26 by 30 October 2026. Companies' annual filings follow their AGM.

05Oct 26

Half-yearly MSME-1 return for April–September due 31 October 2026

Companies that owe micro or small suppliers for more than 45 days must report those dues to the MCA.

28Sep 26

CBDT extends tax audit report to 21 October and audit-case ITRs to 21 November 2026

Circular No. 07/2026 gives non-transfer-pricing audit cases three more weeks for the tax audit report and the return for AY 2026-27.

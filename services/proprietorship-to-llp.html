About Proprietorship to LLP Conversion

Converting to an LLP gives a growing proprietorship limited liability and a partnership structure, without the full compliance load of a private company. We handle the incorporation and the transfer of the existing business.

Conversions move your business into a new legal form while carrying over its assets, contracts and registrations. We plan the sequence so licences and bank accounts follow without gaps.

What you get

  • Limited liability from the date of conversion
  • Lighter ongoing compliance than a private company
  • Existing business continues without disruption

Documents required

  • Proprietorship's PAN, GST & bank statements
  • Proposed designated partners' details
  • Proof of registered office

Exact requirements vary by state and entity. We confirm the final list on the first call.

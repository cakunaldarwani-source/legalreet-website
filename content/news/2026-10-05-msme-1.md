---
title: "Half-yearly MSME-1 return for April–September due 31 October 2026"
date: 2026-10-05
category: Company Law
excerpt: Companies that owe micro or small suppliers for more than 45 days must report those dues to the MCA.
---
Specified companies that have outstanding payments to micro or small enterprises for more than 45 days from acceptance of goods or services must file **Form MSME-1** for the half year April to September 2026 by **31 October 2026**.

The return lists each such supplier, the amount due and the reason for delay. Late payment to MSE suppliers can also affect the deductibility of the expense under income tax law, so it is worth reviewing your payables ageing now.

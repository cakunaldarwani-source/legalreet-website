---
title: "Quarterly TDS and TCS statements for July–September due by 31 October 2026"
date: 2026-10-20
category: TDS
excerpt: The tax audit extension does not cover TDS returns. Q2 statements must still be filed by 31 October to avoid daily late fees.
---
Deductors and collectors must file their TDS and TCS statements for the July to September 2026 quarter by **31 October 2026**. This date has not been extended.

Late filing attracts a fee of Rs 200 per day until the statement is filed, capped at the amount of tax deducted, and penalties can apply beyond one year. Late statements also delay Form 16A and the credit your deductees see in Form 26AS.

Before filing, reconcile challans with deductions, check PANs of all deductees and confirm that lower-deduction certificates are correctly quoted.

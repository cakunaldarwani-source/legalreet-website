---
title: "LLP Form 8 due 30 October; company AOC-4 and MGT-7 run from the AGM date"
date: 2026-10-15
category: Company Law
excerpt: LLPs must file their statement of accounts and solvency for FY 2025-26 by 30 October 2026. Companies' annual filings follow their AGM.
---
Every LLP must file **Form 8** (statement of account and solvency) for FY 2025-26 by **30 October 2026**, unless the Ministry of Corporate Affairs announces an extension. Additional fees accrue for every day of delay.

For companies, the clock starts at the annual general meeting, which for most companies had to be held by 30 September:

- **AOC-4** (financial statements) within 30 days of the AGM.
- **MGT-7 / MGT-7A** (annual return) within 60 days of the AGM.
- **ADT-1** (auditor appointment) within 15 days of the meeting at which the auditor was appointed.

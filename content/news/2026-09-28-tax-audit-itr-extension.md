---
title: "CBDT extends tax audit report to 21 October and audit-case ITRs to 21 November 2026"
date: 2026-09-28
category: Income Tax
excerpt: Circular No. 07/2026 gives non-transfer-pricing audit cases three more weeks for the tax audit report and the return for AY 2026-27.
---
The Central Board of Direct Taxes has issued Circular No. 07/2026 extending two due dates for assessment year 2026-27:

- **Tax audit report** (Form 3CA or 3CB with Form 3CD): from 30 September to **21 October 2026**.
- **Income tax return** for assessees whose accounts must be audited: from 31 October to **21 November 2026**.

The extension applies to companies and other audited assessees who are not required to file a transfer-pricing report. Partners of audited firms also get the later return date.

**Not extended:** transfer-pricing cases (Form 3CEB stays at 31 October and the return at 30 November), quarterly TDS and TCS statements, GST returns and Companies Act filings. Interest for late payment of tax is not waived by the circular, so pay any balance tax early.

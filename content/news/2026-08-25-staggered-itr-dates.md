---
title: "ITR due dates are now staggered: 31 July for salaried, 31 August for non-audit businesses"
date: 2026-08-25
category: Income Tax
excerpt: For AY 2026-27, non-audit business and professional returns (ITR-3 and ITR-4) are due on 31 August instead of 31 July.
---
Income tax return due dates for AY 2026-27 are no longer the same for all non-audit taxpayers:

- **31 July 2026:** individuals without business or professional income, such as salaried taxpayers filing ITR-1.
- **31 August 2026:** business and professional taxpayers whose accounts do not require audit (ITR-3 and ITR-4).
- **Audit cases:** the standard date of 31 October was later extended to 21 November 2026 for covered assessees.

Returns for AY 2026-27 are filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Income-tax Act, 2025 took effect on 1 April 2026 and applies from tax year 2026-27 onwards.
